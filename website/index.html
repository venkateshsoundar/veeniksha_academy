About Veeniksha

Music learning, shaped around the learner.

Founded in 2020, Veeniksha brings out hidden musical talent through flexible, guided and enjoyable Veena learning.

The learning experience is structured yet personal, helping students build technique, confidence and a lasting connection with music.
