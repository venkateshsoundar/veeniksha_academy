About Veeniksha

Music learning, shaped around the learner.

Founded in 2020, Veeniksha is dedicated to bringing out hidden musical talent through flexible, guided and enjoyable Veena learning.

Students learn through a structured yet personal approach that builds technique, confidence and a lasting connection with music.
