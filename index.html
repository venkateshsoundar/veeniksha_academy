About Veeniksha

Music learning, shaped around the learner.

Founded in 2020, Veeniksha was created to bring out hidden musical talent through flexible, guided and enjoyable Veena learning.

The learning experience is structured yet personal, helping students build technique, confidence and a lasting connection with music.

Frequently asked

Questions before you begin?

Here are a few common questions from new learners.

Are classes suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. Veeniksha supports learners from beginner to advanced levels, with lessons adapted to the student’s current ability.

Can students learn from outside India?

Yes. Veeniksha teaches students worldwide through live online classes.

Are weekday and weekend classes available?

Yes. Flexible scheduling includes weekday and weekend options, subject to availability.

Do you help with buying or maintaining a Veena?

Yes. Guidance is available for customized Veena and accessories, global shipping, and basic home maintenance support.
