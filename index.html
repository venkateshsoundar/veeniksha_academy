About Veeniksha Academy

At Veeniksha Veena & Music Academy, we bring the divine beauty of Veena music to students across the globe. Our mission is to nurture and bring out hidden musical talent through flexible, guided and enjoyable learning.

Structured yet personal, our lessons help each learner develop technique, confidence, musical sensitivity and a lasting relationship with the instrument.
