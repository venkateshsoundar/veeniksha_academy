About Veeniksha

Tradition made approachable. Learning made personal.

Founded in 2020, Veeniksha was created to bring out hidden musical talent through flexible, guided and enjoyable Veena learning.

Lessons are structured yet personal, helping each learner develop technique, confidence, musical sensitivity and a lasting relationship with the instrument.
